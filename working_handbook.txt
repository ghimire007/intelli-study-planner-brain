COURSE STUCTURE of Bachelor of Computer Science

(A) Core Subjects (Complete ALL core subject)

- Autumn: CSIT114, CSIT123, CSIT205, CSCI235 (Prereq: CSIT115), CSIT314 (Prereq: CSIT214 AND 12cp at 200-level)
- Spring:  CSIT127, CSIT226, CSCI203 (Prereq: (CSIT110/111) AND (CSIT113/123))
- Autumn/Spring: CSIT110, CSIT115, CSIT121 (Prereq: CSIT110/111/ENGG100), CSIT128, CSIT214 (Prereq: CSIT114)
Note: CSIT314 is NOT core for students commencing 2023 or before.	

Replacements:
- MATH255 or MATH221 replaces CSIT205. 
- CSIT111 replaces CSIT110. 
- CSIT113 replaces CSIT123. 
- If both a core and its replacement are complete, the replacement is an elective.

For example:
- If CSIT113 rather than CSIT123 is complete, the student is deemed to complete CSIT123 (via CSIT113).
- If both CSIT110 and CSIT111 are complete, then CSIT111 is considered an elective.

(B) Core Selection (Complete ONE)

- Spring: CSCI251 (Prereq: CSIT121 or CSIT213)
- Autumn: CSIT213 (Prereq: CSIT110 or CSIT111)
- If both are complete, CSIT213 is an elective.

- Sessions: Annual or Spring/Autumn (two consecutive sessions)
- Prereq: CSIT214 and 18cp of 200-level CSCI/CSIT/ISIT
- Coreq: CSIT226

You MUST follow the steps below to work out the study plan, strictly separating the Analysis Stage from the Planning Stage.

STAGE 1: ANALYSIS (Audit of Completed Credits) (Note: CP for "credit point")

1.1 Determine Commencement Year and Major(s): Identify the student's major(s) and whether they commenced in 2023 or earlier.
1.2 Identify "Complete" Subjects: A subject is complete if the Grade is HD, D, C, P, PS, or S AND the Status is "Complete", or if it is listed as Specified Credit.
1.3 Sum credits for core subjects, identify if one core selection subject is complete (6cp), sum credits based on the chosen major (if any), and sum credits for all other subjects as electives.
1.4 Calculate the sum of complete CP. Note: CSIT321 is 12cp; all other subjects are 6cp.

DOUDBLE CHECK the Stage 1 results, including verifying the credit point calculation and the categorisaion of complete subjects. REPEAT this stage until the results are correct.

STAGE 2: PLANNING (Drafting the Study Plan)

2.1 List outstanding mandatory Core and Major Core subjects not identified in Stage 1. For double majors, include requirements for both. If no major is declared, no major core is required.
2.2 Schedule the identified mandatory subjects in their correct sessions (Autumn or Spring) based on availability.
2.3 Determine a core selection (if not already completed) and schedule it in the available session. 
2.4 Schedule CSIT321 across two consecutive sessions. If the student was enrolled in CSIT321 in the immediate previous session, they must be enrolled in the second part in the current session.
2.5 Organise the planned subjects from Stage 2.1-2.4 such that each session is capped at 4 subjects. For each subject, ensure its prerequisites (if any) are completed/enrolled in a session strictly prior to the subject being taken, and ensure its co-requisites (if any) are completed/enrolled no later than itself.
2.6 Calculate the required total CP: 144 less than the sum of complete CP (which is calculated in Stage 1.4)
2.7 Add electives to ensure the sum of planned CP equals the required total CP. Note: CSIT321 is 12cp; all other subjects are 6cp.

DOUDBLE CHECK the Stage 2 results, including verifying the credit point calculation, the sessions, and the satisfaction of pre/co-requisites for the planned subjects). REPEAT this stage until the results are correct.
