# ECTE427 — Renewable and Distributed Generation

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** ECTE222 or ECTE324
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Winter
  - Wollongong: Autumn
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ECTE427

The aim of this subject is to provide students with an understanding of renewable and embedded energy systems and their integration, operation and control. Topics include: fundamentals of renewable power generation; application of renewable energy resources to reduce greenhouse emissions; role of renewable and embedded generation in the electricity market; economics of renewable and embedded generation for demand side integration; renewable energy resources electrical characteristics and grid integration issues; modelling of renewable resources; micro-grids and a.c. and d.c. power electronics interfacing; energy storage; and impact of multiple renewable energy units on electricty networks in regards to control, protection and quality of supply.
