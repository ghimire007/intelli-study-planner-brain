# MEDI100 — Human Structure and Function

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 100-level
- **Prerequisites:** none
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** General Schedule
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Autumn
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/MEDI100

MEDI100 explores basic concepts of both structure (anatomy) and function (physiology) of the human body. The subject covers basic principles of anatomy and physiology in the areas of skeletal, joint, muscular, nervous, cardiovascular and respiratory systems.Teaching and learning will take place in lectures and the laboratory settings using state of the art resources.
