# ECTE465 — Wireless Communications and IoT

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** MATH283
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Autumn, DXB UG Spring
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ECTE465

This subject will provide fundamentals and advanced knowledge of wireless communication systems, including applications in Internet of Things (IoT) systems. Topics covered include the fundamentals of electromagnetism, antenna analysis and simulation, and antenna impedance matching. The subject also introduces the Friis transmission equation and its applications, multi-path propagation, and path-loss models. Link budget analysis and large-scale and small-scale fading models for wireless and IoT systems are examined. The subject further covers single-carrier systems, spread spectrum techniques and their applications, code division multiple access (CDMA), and RAKE receivers. Multi-carrier systems and orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) are also introduced, together with advanced channel equalisation methods in both time and frequency domains. In addition, the subject presents cellular concepts and system design fundamentals, mobility management, and multi-user communications in wireless systems. It also covers medium access control (MAC) and protocols for wireless networks and IoT systems, as well as emerging multiple access techniques for 5G and future wireless communications supporting large-scale, low-power, and massive IoT connectivity. Students will also gain experience with practical equipment, network simulation tools, case studies and applications of IoT systems.
