# BMEG202 — Bioinstrumentation

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 200-level
- **Prerequisites:** ENGG104 AND ENGG105
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** General Schedule
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/BMEG202

This subject will introduce 1) engineering instrumentation related to measurement of biomedical signals (including EMG and ECG) and 2) the principles of sensing elements, sensors, and actuator technologies. It will cover the design methodology (including circuits, signal analysis and electrical safety) for biomedical instrumentation systems, involving an instrumentation design project. It will also cover the use of bio-instrument in areas such as orthotics, prosthetics, exoskeletons, and rehabilitation and medical robotics.
