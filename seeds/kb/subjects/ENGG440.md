# ENGG440 — Strategic Management of Engineering

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** none
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ENGG440

The subject introduces a range of concepts and tools used in strategic management, as well as approaches guiding the development of technological capabilities at the firm level. Students will familiarise themselves with the strategic issues faced by technology-based organisations and explore how to acquire, nurture and exploit technological capabilities for achieving and sustaining competitive advantage. An appreciation of the nature of competition and the role of technology and innovation in enhancing competitiveness will help Engineers and Technologists make effective decisions towards achieving organisational goals.
