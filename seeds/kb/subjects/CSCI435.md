# CSCI435 — Computer Vision Algorithms and Systems

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** 24cp @CSCI 300 level
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** General Schedule
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Spring, DXB UG Winter
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/CSCI435

This subject is designed to equip students with an understanding of the fundamental concepts and tools required to analyse, design, and implement practical computer vision systems. Topics covered include digital cameras, image processing, image features, shape detection, video processing and analysis, image classification, object detection, image segmentation, and recent advances in the field. The subject will focus on the practical application of conventional and contemporary computer vision technologies, including deep learning, using widely adopted libraries such as OpenCV and other publicly available libraries.
