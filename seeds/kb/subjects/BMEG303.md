# BMEG303 — Biomechanical Basis of Human Movement

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 300-level
- **Prerequisites:** ENGG102 and (PHYS142 or PHYS143)
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/BMEG303

Anatomy and function of the hip, knee and spinal joints; total joint replacement/implants; mechanisms of implant failure and implant design considerations; gait analysis and kinematics; viscoelasticity; anisotropy; mechanical vs material properties.
