# CSCI322 — Systems Administration

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Pre-Requisite:** CSIT127 plus 18 credit points at 200 level
- **Availability:**
  - Liverpool: Spring
  - Singapore Institute of Management: SIM S4
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/CSCI322

This subject offers a comprehensive examination of systems administration. Students will develop the practical skills and theoretical knowledge to effectively design, deploy, and manage network infrastructures and servers. The curriculum covers configuring essential network services, implementing data management technologies, and securing systems against various threats. Emphasising professional practices and analytical skills, the subject prepares students for systems administration roles adaptable to any platform.
