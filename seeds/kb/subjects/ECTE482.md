# ECTE482 — Network Engineering

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** ECTE364
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Summer, DXB UG Winter
  - Wollongong: Autumn
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ECTE482

This subject primarily covers large scale IP networks. In addition to considering architectures and protocols, a key focus will be the development of techniques to assist the design and optimize performance of these networks. Topics will include: ISP architectures; IPv4/IPv6 transition technologies, router architectures, IP lookup algorithms, deep packet inspection, traffic engineering, Quality of Service; MPLS; VLANs; peer to peer networks, and network management.
