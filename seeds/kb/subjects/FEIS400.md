# FEIS400 — Lean Engineering Start Up

- **Credit Points:** 12
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** ECTE250 OR ECTE350 OR ECTE351 OR CSIT321 OR PHIL328 OR BUS 200
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Summer
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/FEIS400

This subject is designed to run in a start up incubator where participants carry on their ECTE350 projects and develop them into a lean start up business. Topics covered include: Business Model Canvas, Value Proposition Design, IP, Customer discovery, marketing, Product Management, Brand Development. PR and Social Media. digital strategy, Finance, Term Sheet negotiations, Leadership, HR and extensive pitch training. In addition a range of other business topics will be covered.
