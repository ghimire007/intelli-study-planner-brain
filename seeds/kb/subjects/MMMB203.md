# MMMB203 — Engineering Practice and Experimental Methods

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 200-level
- **Prerequisites:** ENGG104
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Autumn
  - Wollongong: Autumn
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMMB203

This subject is designed to provide students with practical engineering skills to support learning across the rest of the degree. Instruction on the use of standard machine tools (drill press, lathe, mill and hand tools) and common sensors and instrumentation will be provided to develop a practical understanding of engineering systems, including: manufacturing processes, data collection; data analysis and processing; quantification of error and tolerances; advanced tools and techniques for design and analysis.
