# BMEG306 — Biomaterials and Tissue Engineering

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 300-level
- **Prerequisites:** ENGG103; BMEG301 or MATE204
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** General Schedule
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/BMEG306

The principles of materials science and cell biology in the context of the design of medical implants, artificial organs, and matrices for tissue engineering, surface characterization of biomaterials and analysis of protein adsorption on biomaterials as well as molecular and cellular interactions with biomaterials, tissue and organ regeneration and tissue remodeling following implantation in various organs, design of implants and prostheses based on control of biomaterials-tissue interactions, and a comparative analysis of intact, biodegradable, and bio-replaceable implants with reference to case studies. Examples of engineering tissues for replacing cartilage, bone, tendons, ligaments, skin and liver will be presented.
