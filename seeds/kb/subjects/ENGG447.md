# ENGG447 — Advanced Building Design for Energy Efficiency and Sustainability

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** Completed 96 credit points of Bachelor of Engineering
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** General Schedule
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ENGG447

This subject will cover a range of topics including: performance-based design and passive design principles; fundamental methodologies used in the analysis of energy efficiency in buildings; emerging technologies to improve energy efficiency; human factors that influence building sustainability performance; zero-emission technologies and possible transition pathways. Case studies will be used, including the Solar Decathlon houses, to illustrate these concepts. Students will also be asked to use modelling tools to undertake building performance analysis.
