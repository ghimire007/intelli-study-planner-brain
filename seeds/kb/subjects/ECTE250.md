# ECTE250 — Engineering Design, Management and IoT Applications

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 200-level
- **Prerequisites:** ECTE233 and (ENGG104 or ECTE170) and (ENGG100 or CSCI191 or CSCI114 or CSIT111)
- **Corequisites:** ECTE203 or ECTE212 or ECTE213
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**

  - UOW Malaysia Penang: UOWM Tri 1, UOWM Tri 2, UOWM Tri 3
  - Wollongong: Spring
  - Zhengzhou University: ZZU S2
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ECTE250

This subject consists of a structured team design activity covering the first four phases of a product design cycle: problem definition, concept generation, concept evaluation and selection, and preliminary design. Student teams will undertake projects towards developing engineering solutions that integrate diverse technical knowledge in telecommunications engineering and Internet of Things, electrical and electronic engineering,  and computer and autonomous systems engineering. The team activity will be supplemented by lectures covering topics including communication skills, teamwork, engineering project management, and technical project design and development. Students will complete practical laboratories on system design, simulation and prototyping, Internet of Things sensors and platforms, and embedded systems. Through this subject, student teams will learn the major tools and techniques to effectively manage the design and development of practical engineering projects and applications.
