# CSCI444 — Perception, Planning and Interactions

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** 24cp @300 level
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/CSCI444

This subject is designed to provide a solid foundation in perception, planning and interactions in embodied artificial intelligence using accessible tools and programming languages. Emphasis is placed on hands-on learning through simulation, allowing students to experiment and apply theoretical concepts. By the end of the course, students will have a fundamental understanding of embodied artificial intelligence and be able to design and analyse algorithms related to how embodied intelligent agents perceive their environments, make decisions, and interact with humans and environments through advanced sensors and technologies such as multimodal generative artificial intelligence.
