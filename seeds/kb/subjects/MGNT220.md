This subject examines four theoretical frames that are used to analyse organisations. Students develop an understanding of theories and concepts that explain how organisational members are affected by organisational structures, human resource flows, power and politics, cultures, and symbols. Emphasis is placed on using these complementary frames to analyse real organisations.
