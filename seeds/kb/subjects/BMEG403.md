# BMEG403 — Biomedical Devices, Regulations and Ethics

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** BMEG202 AND BMEG301
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/BMEG403

This subject will explore 1) operational principles and design challenges, 2) regulation of medical devices in Australia and overseas, and 3) the application of ethics principles in the design, manufacture and commercialisation of biomedical devices.
Adherence to these fundamental principles will lead to the successful and sustainable delivery of products as related to market-specific ethical processes and procedures, clinical protocols and technology standards, safety compliance and regulatory guidelines.
