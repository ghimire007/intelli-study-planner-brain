# ACCY112 — Accounting In Organisations

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Pre-Requisite:** ACCY111
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ACCY112

The subject advances understanding of accounting in organisations. The subject introduces accounting for complex equity structures, and develops the theoretical and technical aspects of accounting for assets and the protection of assets through internal controls. Accounting for the past and future is examined through the introduction of cost structures and their application in solving fundamental business problems using cost-volume profit analysis. The application of budgets is explored.
