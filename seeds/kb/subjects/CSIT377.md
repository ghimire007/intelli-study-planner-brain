# CSIT377 — Enterprise Cloud Development

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Pre-Requisite:** CSIT128 and 6 credit points at 200 level
- **Co-Requisite:** 12 credit points at 200 level
- **Availability:**
  - Liverpool: Spring
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/CSIT377

Cloud Computing is the on-demand delivery of computational resources like hardware infrastructure, applications, servers, storage, databases, networking, and software over the Internet. This subject aims to provide fundamental knowledge and applicable hands-on skills for enterprise-level Cloud Computing. The subject covers various cloud deployment models (public, private, hybrid) and service models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, serverless), enabling a critical understanding of how different cloud solutions are structured and delivered. Students are exposed to ready-to-implement solutions for enterprise IT systems through common cloud computing technologies that enable microservice architecture, Web APIs, mashup software, and similar applications. The subject provides hands-on experience in designing and developing scalable, reliable, and secure cloud solutions using a mainstream cloud computing platform.
