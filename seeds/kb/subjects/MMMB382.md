# MMMB382 — Manufacturing Engineering Principles

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 300-level
- **Prerequisites:** ENGG103 or MMMB201
- **Corequisites:** ENGG251
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Autumn
  - Wollongong: Autumn
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMMB382

This subject introduces students to the fundamental principles of manufacturing engineering. Topics include an overall perspective on manufacturing, and interactions between product design, materials and manufacturing processes. Students will study casting and forming theory and processes; machining theory and processes; joining and assembly processes; non-conventional processes; computer integrated processes; additive manufacturing; process capability and quality control; manufacturing economics; and emerging trends in advanced manufacturing.
