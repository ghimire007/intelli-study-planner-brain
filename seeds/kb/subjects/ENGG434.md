# ENGG434 — Introduction to Materials Welding and Joining

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** ENGG103 Materials in Design
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ENGG434

The subject introduces the student to the selection and cost effective application of joining technology. OH&amp;S and quality issues and recent welding innovations are covered.
