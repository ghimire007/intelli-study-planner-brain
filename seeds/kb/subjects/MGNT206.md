This subject is concerned with the importance of strategically aligning the management of human resources with the organisation’s business objectives in order to achieve and sustain a competitive advantage. Emphasis is placed on how the concepts and techniques of contemporary strategic HRM can be applied in organisations to facilitate the acquisition and development of staff, to influence positively their job performance, and to manage the processes of staff turnover and retention.
