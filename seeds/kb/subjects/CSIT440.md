# CSIT440 — Research Methodology

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** Unknown
- **Prerequisites:** 24 credit points CSCI, CSIT, ISIT @ 300 level
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Autumn
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/CSIT440

This subject introduces students to research methodology. Topics include defining research problems, research design, conducting a systematic literature review, methods of data collection, processing and analysis of data, writing a research proposal, and interpretation and report writing. Students will gain understanding of different research methodology, including qualitative and quantitative analysis (e.g., causal model and statistical analysis). Students will learn how to design an appropriate research plan.
