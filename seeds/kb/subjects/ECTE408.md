# ECTE408 — Image Processing for Autonomous Systems

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** none
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Autumn
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ECTE408

This subject offers a dynamic and practical exploration of artificial intelligence and its transformative roles in machine vision. Through a blend of foundational theories and hands-on applications, students will gain an in-depth understanding of digital image processing and cutting-edge AI techniques. The subject emphasises core image processing principles and their integration with deep learning to solve real-world problems involving image and video analysis. The course combines academic rigour with practical relevance, preparing students for research and industrial applications in image and video processing. Topics covered include: an overview of AI and deep learning, deep neural networks, image processing in spatial/transform domains, image filtering and enhancements, object detection, image segmentation and classification, and machine vision applications.
