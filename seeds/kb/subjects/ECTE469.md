# ECTE469 — Queuing Theory and Optimization

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** MATH283 or STAT231
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** General Schedule
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Winter
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ECTE469

This subject will provide the essential theoretical and practical foundations for modelling, designing and optimising systems.  The first part will cover the fundamentals of queuing theory. It will include the basic properties of stochastic processes such as the Poisson process, discrete and continuous time Markov chains.  It will then cover how these processes are used to model systems with a single and multiple queues.  Students will learn how to derive key performance metrics such as the average waiting time, number of users and the blocking probability of a system.  The second part will cover the basics of optimization theory.  It will cover unconstrained and constrained optimisation methods involving discrete or/and continuous decision variables.   Further, the subject will cover how optimization and queuing theory are used in various engineering disciplines.
