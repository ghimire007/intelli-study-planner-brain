# ECTE233 — Digital Hardware

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 200-level
- **Prerequisites:** CSCI191 or ECTE171 or ENGG100 or ENGG104
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Autumn
  - UOW Malaysia Penang: UOWM Tri 1, UOWM Tri 2, UOWM Tri 3
  - Wollongong: Autumn
  - Zhengzhou University: ZZU S1
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ECTE233

Topics covered in this subject include: combinational logic, simplification of logic expressions, Karnaugh maps; sequential logic, flip-flops, registers, clock, timing and synchronisation problems; sequential machines, Mealy and Moore machines, timing diagrams and state tables; and programmable logic array and programmable logic controllers.
