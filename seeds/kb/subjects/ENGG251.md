# ENGG251 — Mechanics of Solids

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 200-level
- **Prerequisites:** ENGG152 or ENGG102
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Dubai: DXB UG Autumn, DXB UG Winter
  - Wollongong: Autumn
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ENGG251

Stress on a section, concept of stress-strain relationship and Hooke&#39;s Law. Torsion of shafts and hollow sections. Problems in bending and stress of beams. Analysis of plane stress and plane strain, combined stresses. Elasticity and plasticity for metals, and inelastic behaviour of non metals. Failure theories. Beam deflections and simple column buckling. Thermal stresses and strain energy concept. Experimental techniques. Recommended minimum preparation is Engineering Mechanics (Statics), Engineering Mathematics and Engineering Materials.
