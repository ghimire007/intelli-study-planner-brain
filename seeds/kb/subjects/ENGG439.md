# ENGG439 — Engineering Logistics and Operations Management

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** none
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Spring
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ENGG439

Engineers are required to make strategically significant decisions that could substantially enhance an organisation’s overall success or competitiveness. This subject aims at helping students appreciate the role of the operations function within organisations and the significance of logistics in ensuring swift and even flow of material through supply chains. It covers the key aspects of operations and logistics systems design, operation and optimisation within the broader context of supply network configuration. The subject introduces a range of quantitative tools and techniques that support managerial decision-making and helps students develop the skills required to solve industrial problems using process mapping, analysis and simulation modelling.
