# MMMB499 — Thesis B

- **Credit Points:** 18
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of 120cps, with a weighted average mark of 75 or above
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** No restrictions/exclusions f; this subject.
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** none
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Annual, Spring/Autumn
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMMB499

As an alternative to MMMB498, subject MMMB499 (18 credit points) may be taken by students in the Engineering Scholars program, or by other high achieving students with the permission of the Academic Program Director. A student electing to take MMMB499 will undertake a longer period of work and complete a longer thesis.
Students are expected to spend 504 hours on the 18 credit point thesis. The thesis is a core element of the degree in each engineering course. The knowledge and skills acquired in the design, experimentation, analysis, management, and communications aspects of the course are brought together in an individual project undertaken by the student under the guidance of an academic supervisor. Specific disciplines will advise further requirements at the start of the thesis.
