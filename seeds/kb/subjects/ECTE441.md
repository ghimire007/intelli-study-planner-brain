# ECTE441 — Intelligent Control

- **Credit Points:** 6
- **Subject Level:** 400-level
- **Prerequisites:** MATH201 or MATH283
- **Corequisites:** none
- **Exclusions:** none
- **Degree Restrictions:** none
- **Tags / Topic Areas:** General Schedule
- **Availability:**
  - Wollongong: Autumn
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/subjects/2026/ECTE441

This subject will review the latest control techniques used where the system is poorly known or changing with time or conditions. Methods examined in detail may include: fuzzy systems, neural networks, adaptive control, crisp and neuro fuzzy control.
