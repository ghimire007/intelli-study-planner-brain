# MAJ44307 — Artificial Intelligence and Big Data and Cyber Security

- **Credit Points:** 48
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/aos/2026/MAJ44307

The Artificial Intelligence and Big Data and Cyber Security fields represent critical and complementary domains in today’s digital landscape, addressing challenges across science, society, and business. Artificial Intelligence (AI) focuses on simulating intelligence using computers, often relying on the analysis of massive datasets, commonly referred to as Big Data. Together, these fields enable innovative solutions to complex problems, from population growth and medical technology to transport, trade, and security. Meanwhile, Cyber Security ensures the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of the systems and data that AI and Big Data rely upon, protecting them from unauthorised access and digital threats.

As modern AI depends on large-scale data, professionals with expertise in data mining, visualisation, analysis, and discovery are essential for extracting meaningful insights from complex datasets. These skills are vital for driving strategic decisions and innovation in sectors like healthcare, commerce, and public policy. Equally, the growth of online social, consumer, and business activities has increased the need for Cyber Security professionals who can secure our digital environment and economy. From safeguarding personal information to defending essential services, Cyber Security plays a pivotal role in ensuring a safe and reliable digital future.

Cyber Security is a global priority, with high demand across law enforcement, government, defence, and finance sectors. Similarly, the combined expertise in AI and Big Data is integral to solving global issues and advancing industries through intelligent systems. This comprehensive program covers cutting-edge topics such as blockchain, cryptocurrency, multimedia security, Internet of Things (IoT) security, obfuscation, and reverse engineering. Students also gain practical skills in penetration testing, ethical hacking certification, and data manipulation, equipping them for careers at the forefront of innovation and defence.

Graduates will be uniquely prepared to meet the challenges of an interconnected digital world, where securing data and systems is as crucial as analysing and utilising them to drive progress. With opportunities spanning diverse industries, this program offers a future-focused pathway to impactful and rewarding careers.
