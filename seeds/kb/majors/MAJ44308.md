# MAJ44308 — Digital Systems Security and Artificial Intelligence and Big Data

- **Credit Points:** 48
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/aos/2026/MAJ44308

The Artificial Intelligence and Big Data and Digital Systems Security fields are at the forefront of addressing critical challenges in science, society, and business. Artificial Intelligence (AI) focuses on simulating intelligence using computers, while Big Data is concerned with processing datasets that are too large and complex for traditional methods. These complementary fields work together, as modern AI relies on vast quantities of data to achieve its objectives. Combined, they provide innovative solutions to pressing global issues such as population growth, transport, security, medical technology, trade, and commerce.

As digital transformation accelerates, information and systems security have become essential to everyday life. Protecting digital identities, assets, and sensitive data is crucial not only for individuals but also for businesses, governments, and industries. Whether securing personal email and online privacy or safeguarding critical infrastructure in sectors such as banking, finance, insurance, retail, and defence, the demand for skilled professionals in these areas continues to grow.

Students in this program will develop expertise in leveraging AI and Big Data to uncover strategic insights through data mining, visualisation, analysis, manipulation, and discovery. They will also gain the knowledge needed to secure digital information and systems, ensuring data integrity and privacy in an era of increasing cyber threats. These combined skills position graduates to play vital roles in advancing industries, protecting sensitive information, and shaping a secure and intelligent digital future.

With strong demand across sectors for expertise in AI, Big Data, and Digital Systems Security, this program offers a future-ready pathway to innovative and impactful careers.
