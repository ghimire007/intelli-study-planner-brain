# MAJ44306 — Digital Systems Security and Cyber Security

- **Credit Points:** 48
- **Handbook URL:** https://courses.uow.edu.au/aos/2026/MAJ44306

The Cyber Security & Digital Systems Security double major equips students with the essential knowledge and skills to safeguard our increasingly interconnected world against unauthorised access, digital threats, and security breaches. As society becomes more reliant on digital technologies for social, consumer, and business activities, there is a growing need for professionals who can protect critical data and systems while ensuring online privacy and security.

This double major combines the technical and theoretical foundations of cyber security with the practical applications of digital systems security. Students will explore advanced topics, such as blockchain, cryptocurrency, multimedia security, Internet of Things (IoT) security, reverse engineering, and obfuscation. Hands-on learning in penetration testing and preparation for ethical hacking certification ensures graduates are ready to address real-world security challenges.

In parallel, the program focuses on digital systems security, where students will learn to secure personal and organisational data, digital identities, and assets across a wide range of applications. With growing vulnerabilities in sectors such as banking, finance, insurance, retail, government, and defence, students will gain the expertise to design, implement, and manage robust systems that protect sensitive information and maintain digital trust.

Graduates of this program will be uniquely positioned to address strategic priorities in cyber and digital systems security. Whether preventing disruptions to essential services, safeguarding national infrastructure, or protecting personal data from misuse, they will play a pivotal role in securing the digital future across industries. With strong demand for expertise in this field, this double major offers an exciting pathway to impactful and future-ready careers.
