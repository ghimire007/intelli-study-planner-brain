# Credit for Prior Learning (CPL)

If you have completed previous studies or have relevant professional experience, you can request course credit or subject exemptions to help reduce the duration and overall cost of your course.

Credit for prior learning (CPL) is an assessment process: getting credit for previously completed study, or relevant work experience, related to the subjects covered in your chosen course. UOW recognises different types of prior learning regardless of where and how it was achieved.

**Current students** — already commenced at UOW — apply via the AskUOW CPL process described below.

**Future students** — planning to study at UOW — request CPL assessment during the admissions application (Step 5 of the application portal, "Apply Online"). UAC applicants should refer to current-student application-for-credit information instead.

## Eligibility

Current students can apply for credit based on previous study/learning if they can:

1. Demonstrate how the study/learning meets UOW subject or course learning outcomes, and
2. Provide evidence of the previous study/learning.

Important considerations:

- Approved credit may affect your Weighted Average Mark (WAM) and eligibility to graduate with distinction.
- Applying does not guarantee approval — applications go through an academic eligibility assessment against the Credit for Prior Learning policy and course design procedures.
- Credit is only approved up to the maximum credit points permitted at each level of study for your degree type, per the Course design procedures.
- Approved credit is either:
  - **Specified** — direct equivalence between the prior learning and a UOW subject's learning outcomes.
  - **Unspecified** — no direct subject equivalence, but may count toward elective credit points (if your degree has elective options).

## When to submit an application

- No later than **3 weeks before** the start of session, if seeking credit for a subject you are *currently* enrolled in (see the Key Dates webpage for enrolment dates).
- As soon as possible, for subjects you'll need to enrol into in upcoming sessions — to leave time to plan your enrolment decisions.

Processing times:

- Peak periods (after Release of Results, before next session starts): 4–6 weeks
- Off-peak (during session, up to Release of Results): 1–3 weeks

Keep enrolment/withdrawal/financial-liability key dates in mind when deciding on credit applications.

## International students

- Approved CPL may affect course duration, Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE), and student visa status.
- Seek academic advice on completion-date implications before applying (book via AskUOW).
- Under the ESOS Act, UOW must notify the Department of Home Affairs (DHA) of any course duration changes.
- Contact DHA on 131 881 for visa-specific questions.
- Under-18 students need parent/guardian permission — contact AskUOW for the International Student Guardianship Form.

## Types of prior learning and how to apply

The application process differs by type of prior learning:

**Formal learning** — part of a structured program leading to full/partial achievement of an officially accredited qualification at a tertiary institution (university, TAFE, private college). See "How do I apply for credit for prior formal learning?" (no confirmed URL — do not link).

**Informal & non-formal learning:**

- Informal learning — gained through work, social, or volunteer experience; not formally organised in terms of objectives, time, or learning support.
- Non-formal learning — structured but does not lead to an officially accredited qualification (e.g. MOOCs, micro-credentials, employer training programs).

See "How do I apply for credit for prior informal and non-formal learning?" (no confirmed URL — do not link).
