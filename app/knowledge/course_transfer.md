# Can I change courses?

**Source:** [AskUOW — "Can I change courses?"](https://uowprod.service-now.com/askuow/en/can-i-change-courses?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0012619)

**Contacts:**

- AskUOW — [askuow@uow.edu.au](mailto:askuow@uow.edu.au) — submit your Course Transfer application form here

---

Once you have completed a session of study in your original course, you may be eligible to apply to transfer into a different course.

If you are a new student and have not yet commenced your course, contact the Future Students team before session commences for advice on how to proceed.

If you have already enrolled in subjects under your current degree and no longer want to complete these, refer to the key deadlines for subject withdrawal and contact the Future Students team for advice.

If you want to stay in the same course but transfer to a different campus, see the campus transfer topic instead.

## Before you apply

**1. Find your preferred course** — browse the Course Finder for entry requirements ("Admissions information") and intake sessions ("Key dates").

**2. Understand eligibility** — assessed against the new course's entry requirements and an academic readiness assessment (grades/WAM, reason for transfer, performance in related subjects). Guide-only WAM benchmarks:

| Transfer situation                                         | Current WAM (guide only) |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Transferring to a degree with a similar entry threshold      | Passing WAM at minimum    |
| Transferring to a degree with a higher entry threshold       | 65–70 WAM                 |
| Transferring to an advanced degree from a standard degree     | 80–85+ WAM                |
| Transferring to a degree with a lower entry threshold         | Case-by-case              |

Some degrees/campuses have limited places — entry is competitive.

**3. Understand the fees** — you're charged the new course's fees for the year you commence transfer. Scholarship holders should check their scholarship terms and contact the Scholarships team (see above). Sponsored students need written sponsor permission attached to the application. See the Fees and Assistance webpage for details.

**4. Plan for eligible credit** — you'll be assessed for specified/unspecified credit from subjects already completed at UOW. Outstanding cross-institutional/exchange results must be finalised first. See the credit/RPL topic for more detail. Subject makeup of each course is in the Course Handbook.

**5. Prepare evidence of academic readiness** — Education course applicants must attach a teaching questionnaire. UOW academic performance is considered automatically (no need to provide evidence of it yourself); optional extra evidence includes transcripts, subject outlines, training certificates, CV, references.

## Applying

Submit the Course Transfer application form to AskUOW via [askuow@uow.edu.au](mailto:askuow@uow.edu.au). Applications close **two weeks before session starts**.

Processing time:

- Domestic students: ~2 weeks (up to 5 weeks during peak periods, e.g. after Release of Results)
- International students: ~4 weeks (up to 8 weeks during peak periods)

## Outcomes

The Faculty may approve or decline. They may contact you for more information, or advise on what to do before reapplying (e.g. additional study, target grades).

**Domestic students:** if approved, current course status becomes "transfer," new course becomes "active," and you can enrol in the new course.

**International students:** if approved, you'll be emailed a new offer detailing any credit awarded. Sign and return it; once accepted, a new Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) is issued. Course status updates the same way as above once the new course is active.

## International students on a student visa

- An approved transfer may affect course duration, CoE, and visa status.
- Under the ESOS Act, UOW must notify the Department of Home Affairs of course duration changes.
- Contact the Department of Home Affairs (131 881) for visa-specific questions.
- Under-18 students need parent/guardian permission (International Student Guardianship Form via AskUOW).
- Not all course/campus combinations are available to student-visa holders — check Course Finder.
- If you need a visa extension within 30 days of transfer, request your CoE urgently by emailing [esos-enquiries@uow.edu.au](mailto:esos-enquiries@uow.edu.au) with your student number and subject line "Urgent CoE update - course transfer".
