Students who wish to withdraw from a subject should read this carefully first: depending on **when** you withdraw, there may be financial and/or academic penalties.

**Important:** withdrawing from a tutorial or class within a subject does NOT withdraw you from the subject itself. You must follow the process below to withdraw fully.

HDR students should contact the Graduate Research School (see above) instead of following the standard process.

## Withdrawal timing, process, and outcomes

**1. On or before the session's census date** (see Key Dates page for each session's census date):

- Process: Log in to SOLS → "Enrolment and Variations" → click the red "Withdraw" button against the subject. Repeat per subject.
- Outcome: "Withdrawn" status; removed from academic transcript; **not liable for the subject fee**.
- If withdrawing from all subjects for the session, apply for a Leave of Absence.

**2. After census date but before the last date to withdraw without academic penalty** (see Key Dates page):

- Process: same SOLS self-service steps as above.
- Outcome: "Withdrawn" status; removed from transcript; **you remain liable for subject fees**.
- If withdrawing from all subjects, apply for a Leave of Absence.

**3. After the last date to withdraw without academic penalty, up to 10 working days after the subject's result release** (see Key Dates page):

- Process: cannot self-withdraw via SOLS — complete the Subject Management form instead. Only considered under Compassionate and Compelling circumstances (see below).
- Outcome: "Approved Withdrawal" status; removed from transcript; you remain liable for subject fees.
- If withdrawing from all subjects, apply for a Leave of Absence.

**4. 10 or more working days after result release** (see Key Dates page):

- Process: first make an appointment with your Head of Students for academic advice (email askuow@uow.edu.au to arrange), then complete the Subject Management form.
- Only considered in exceptional circumstances that prevented an earlier withdrawal application.
- Outcome: "Approved Withdrawal" status; removed from transcript; you remain liable for subject fees.
- If withdrawing from all subjects, apply for a **retrospective** Leave of Absence.

## Compassionate and Compelling circumstances

Assessed case-by-case; supporting documentation is required with the application. Examples include (not exhaustive):

- Medical illness or injury preventing participation in studies
- Witnessing a serious accident, or being the victim of a serious crime
- Unexpectedly taking up caring responsibilities for a close family member
- A situation where studies can't be participated in and no additional learning opportunities can be offered

See the Compassionate and Compelling Circumstances Guidelines for more detail.

## Fee refunds / HELP remission

If approved for a late withdrawal on compassionate/compelling grounds, you are **still liable for the subject fee** — upfront payments are forfeited, and HELP-deferred fees are added to your cumulative HELP debt.

After an approved late withdrawal, you may be eligible for a fee refund / HECS-FEE-HELP remission if you can demonstrate special circumstances that:

- Were beyond your control, and
- Did not occur (or their full impact wasn't known) until after the census date, and
- Made it impracticable to complete your study requirements.

Refer to the domestic or international refunds information (as applicable) for eligibility and how to apply.
