The Bachelor of Computer Science (Dean's Scholar) (course 1802, 144 CP, 3 years
full-time, Wollongong only) is an enriched version of the Bachelor of Computer
Science (766) for high-achieving students. Students complete the normal degree
requirements plus a 400-level Dean's Scholar subject, get direct access to
academic mentoring, and are encouraged to continue into Honours and research.
Places are limited each year.

## Getting in

- **From school:** ATAR-SR 95. Early Admission interviews are offered to Year 12
  students with 90% in Mathematics OR 85% in Mathematics Extension 1 or 2, AND
  80% in English Standard OR 75% in English Advanced.
- **Transferring from 766 (current UOW students):** available to students who
  have completed **48 CP** of the equivalent non-scholar degree **within one year**
  with a **minimum WAM of 80**. See the course_transfer topic for the transfer
  process itself.

## Staying in

Students must **maintain an average of 80% in each year of study**. If the
average falls below 80%, the case is assessed individually and the student may
be transferred into the non-Dean's Scholar equivalent degree (766).

## How it differs from 766

- Same core, core selection, majors, capstone (CSIT321) and 60 CP 100-level cap.
- Adds **6 CP** from the Dean's Scholar list (all 400-level, each needing 18–24 CP
  of 300-level study first): CSCI471, CSCI426, CSCI433, CSCI435, CSIT470,
  CSCI427, CSCI444.
- In 2026 the 1802 core lists **MATH255** where 766 lists CSIT205.
