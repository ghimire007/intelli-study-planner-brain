The Bachelor of Computer Science (Honours) (course 765) is a **follow-on degree**
taken after the Bachelor of Computer Science: one extra year full-time, 48 CP,
Wollongong on-campus only. It builds research skills and prepares students for
higher research degrees.

## Getting in

- Qualified for a bachelor's degree (AQF 7 or recognised overseas equivalent) in a
  relevant field, with a **minimum WAM of 75%**, and
- **at least two relevant final-year subjects** with a final grade of **75% or higher**.
- Talk to the Program Coordinator well in advance to discuss the program and
  negotiate a thesis topic and supervisors.

## What you study (48 CP)

- **Core (30 CP):** CSIT440 Research Methodology (6 CP) + CSIT499 Honours Research Project (24 CP).
- **Electives (18 CP):** three 400-level subjects from the 765 elective list. With the
  Academic Program Director's permission, up to 6 CP can be swapped for a 300-level
  Computer Science subject or a 400-level subject from another discipline.

## How the honours class is calculated — Method 1 (used by 765)

Only **400-level and Honours-level subjects** count (weight 1); 100–300-level
subjects are weighted 0. For 765 that means the honours WAM is the credit-point
weighted average of the 48 CP honours-year subjects.

| Class | Honours WAM |
|---|---|
| Class I | 85% to 100% |
| Class II, Division 1 | 75% to less than 85% |
| Class II, Division 2 | 65% to less than 75% |
| Class III | 50% to less than 65% |

Honours WAM = Σ(mark × CP) ÷ Σ(CP), over the honours-year subjects.
