We understand that circumstances change, and that studies are not always the biggest priority in a student's life. Before deciding to leave UOW, follow these steps for tailored support.

## Step 1: Contact a Student Support Coordinator

They can help with questions about studies, academic support, or referrals to other University/local community services. Available at all UOW campuses and faculties.

## Step 2: Consider alternatives to withdrawing

- **Leave of Absence (LOA)** — take time off instead of ceasing studies entirely. Eligibility and periods depend on whether the student is Domestic or International.
- **Full-time vs part-time study** — changing study load may suit a student's situation better than withdrawing. Eligibility/rules differ for Domestic, International, and sponsored students.
- **UOW support services** — academic and personal support, including help transitioning into study and beyond.

## Step 3: Complete the UOW exit survey

Before or after withdrawing, students are encouraged to complete a short exit survey covering their experience and reasons for leaving.

## Step 4: Withdraw from all subjects in SOLS

A student must withdraw from all enrolled subjects in SOLS before UOW registers them as withdrawn.

## Domestic students

**Leaving before census date:** If on a HECS-HELP loan, the student must withdraw from all subjects (in SOLS) before the session's census date, to avoid failed grades and ensure debts are reported correctly to the ATO.

**Leaving after census date (no LOA submitted before census):** Enrolment/financial liability depends on timing:

- Before week 9 of session — apply to Remove Subject without Academic Penalty; if approved, may apply for a domestic fee refund (special circumstances only).
- After release of results — apply to Amend Academic Record; if approved, may apply for a domestic fee refund (special circumstances only).

If a student neither withdraws nor enrols in the next session (without an official LOA), their course lapses. Returning after a lapse requires applying for a new offer.

**Next steps:** discuss in person at Student Central, call 1300 275 869, or email askuow@uow.edu.au. Consider completing the exit survey regardless of the final decision.

## International students

Refer to the "Student visa compliance, leaving UOW" resource for visa-specific considerations before leaving.

**Important:** Failing to seek official withdrawal from subjects can cause the course to lapse, which may result in non-compliance with student visa conditions set by the Department of Home Affairs.
