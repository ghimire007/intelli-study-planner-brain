# Can I transfer to a different campus?

Source: AskUOW — "Can I transfer to a different campus?"

You can apply to transfer to another UOW campus while remaining in your current course, provided that:

1. Your course is offered at your preferred campus.
2. Your course and campus accept intakes for your session of transfer.
3. If there are capacity limits, these have not been reached.

Use the Course Finder to check where a course is offered and when it accepts intakes. Some campuses have limited places, so entry for transfer students is competitive.

Not all course/campus combinations are available to international students on a student visa — check Course Finder.

To request a campus transfer, use the Course Transfer application form (same form used for course transfers).
